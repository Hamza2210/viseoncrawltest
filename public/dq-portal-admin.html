Your organisation's tags

This is the list people choose from when tagging a dataset. Tick restricts to make a tag an access control: a dataset carrying it is then visible only to members granted it. Admins always see everything. A colour is worn everywhere the tag appears; auto keeps the colour made from its name.

Who holds what

Grant each member the restricting tags they may see. Saved as you click. A member granted none sees only datasets that no restricting tag covers.

On-premises Agents
